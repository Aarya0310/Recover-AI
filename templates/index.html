11. Metric Definitions & Simulation Methodology

Standardized financial metrics and evaluation standards for operators & judges

Revenue at Risk

Sum of transaction amounts currently at risk from unresolved failed payments awaiting intelligent recovery.

Potentially Recoverable

Volume of currently failed transactions whose AI recovery likelihood meets or exceeds the configured policy threshold (≥75%).

Expected Recovery

Probability-weighted expected value calculated as Amount × Recovery Probability across eligible failed transactions.

Recovered Revenue

Total volume of payments successfully captured and settled into the merchant ledger by the simulated recovery agent.

Incremental Revenue

Net additional financial recovery gained by RecoverAI's strategy engine over the naive blind-retry baseline (RecoverAI − Baseline).

Recovery Rate

Percentage of total checkout volume successfully salvaged (Recovered Transactions ÷ Total Transactions × 100).
